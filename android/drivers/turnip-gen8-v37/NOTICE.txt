Turnip Gen8 V37 (Mesa Turnip, the open-source Vulkan driver for Adreno GPUs)

Built by StevenMXZ from Mesa 26.3.0-devel (git a995eaeda4) and distributed
as an adrenotools driver package ("Turnip Gen8 V37", package version 7).
Pinyon Shift bundles it unmodified as the recommended driver for Adreno 7xx
GPUs; android_gpu_driver chooses another driver or the system's.

Mesa source: https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa
